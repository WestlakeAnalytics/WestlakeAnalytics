Everyone loves an underdog story. It's part of what keeps us coming back to sporting events over and over again. In rugby, a sport often considered to be dominated by a few very strong teams, this feeling is strong. Since the notion of World Rankings was introduced in 2003, Tier 1 teams (Argentina, Australia, England, France, Ireland, Italy, New Zealand, Scotland, South Africa, Wales) have featured in 1,543 internationals. Roughly 1 in 5 of these have been won by the underdog.

Yellow to purple shows how many places apart the teams were in an upset; grey is any other game.
